Being found for relevant searches

Local SEO is about matching real search intent. We analyse the services people search for, the pages already gaining impressions and the gaps that prevent Google from understanding the business clearly.

Technical and on-page optimisation

We review indexability, titles, descriptions, headings, canonicals, structured data, sitemap coverage and internal links. Changes are prioritised according to evidence rather than made simply to satisfy an SEO checklist.

Content with a purpose

We create or improve pages that answer useful questions and explain services in enough depth to be helpful. We avoid repetitive location pages or keyword stuffing that adds no value to users.

Google Business and local presence

Website information and Google Business Profile should reinforce each other. Services, contact details, business information and local relevance are more useful when they remain consistent across channels.

How long does local SEO take?

There is no guaranteed timeframe. New domains and competitive searches can take time. We monitor impressions, queries, positions, crawl status and clicks to understand whether Google is discovering and testing the pages.

Does Google Business replace website SEO?

No. Google Business supports local discovery and Maps visibility, while the website provides deeper service information, content and crawlable pages that can build organic visibility over time.

Related services

Local SEO can be combined with website development, Google Business optimisation, social media and digital advertising depending on the goals of the business.

Local visibility for Algarve businesses

Local search behaviour can vary significantly by sector and season. Tourism, accommodation, restaurants, retail and professional services may depend on different queries and customer journeys. We use Search Console data and the actual structure of the business to prioritise pages and topics, then review how Google responds before making further changes.
