FEATURED PROJECT

Sun House Algarve

We listened to the client. Simplified management. Built a tailored digital presence.

The project began with meetings to understand what Sun House needed: to present its services clearly, build trust with property owners and make finding holiday accommodation in the Algarve easier. We explored the business needs and organised the information to create a simple, coherent experience aligned with the brand’s identity.

From that groundwork, we developed a website in Portuguese, French and English bringing together accommodation, property management, maintenance and renovation services. The challenge

Sun House needed a website that brought together its three business areas: holiday accommodation, property management, and maintenance and renovation. The information had to be clear and accessible to different audiences. It had to build trust with property owners, help guests find the right accommodation and present the services consistently with the brand’s identity. Through meetings with the client, we explored their goals and day-to-day management needs to define a structure that connected the visitor experience with the team’s work.

What we built

We developed a website in Portuguese, French and English, with navigation in each language, property listings, photo galleries, accommodation features and stay enquiries. To support daily operations, we built an admin dashboard for managing properties, pricing, availability and booking requests, alongside a centralised calendar and iCal synchronisation. We also worked on the website’s SEO, focusing on page structure, content and service presentation to support search visibility and Sun House’s digital presence in the Algarve. The result is a tailored digital presence that showcases Sun House’s services and gives the team greater independence to keep information up to date and manage the business.
